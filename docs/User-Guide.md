# User Guide

Assalamu alaikum, and welcome to Al Quran Quotes.

Al Quran Quotes is a small Android app. Each day it shows you one ayah (verse) from the Quran. You see the Arabic text, an English translation, and the name and number of the surah and ayah.

The app is simple and quiet. It works without internet, it asks for no permissions, and it collects no data about you.

## Quick facts

- One ayah each day, from a set of 33 ayahs inside the app.
- A new ayah comes at midnight, in your local time.
- Works fully offline.
- Needs Android 7.0 or newer.
- Not on Google Play yet.
