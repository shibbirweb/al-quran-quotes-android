# Settings

Tap **Settings** in the bar at the bottom of the screen to open this tab. The title **Settings** is at the top in large letters, and it shrinks into a smaller bar when you scroll down.

## What it shows today

The Settings tab is a first look. It shows some information, but nothing here can be changed yet:

- **Theme**: Follows your phone. The app is dark or light like your phone. See [Reading comfort](User-Reading-Comfort.md).
- **Text size**: Follows your phone. Change the font size in your phone's settings to make the text bigger or smaller.
- **About**: the app's name and the version you have installed.

At the bottom it says "More settings are coming soon."

## Coming later

We plan to let you choose the app's theme and the size of the Arabic text here. See [Coming soon](User-Coming-Soon.md).
