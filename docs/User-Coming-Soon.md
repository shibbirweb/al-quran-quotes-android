# Coming Soon

These features are **planned, but not available yet**. They are ideas we want to add in the future. We cannot promise a date, and the details may change.

## Planned features

- **Favorites**: save the quotes you love and see them in a list.
- **Share an ayah**: send an ayah to friends and family through your other apps, as text and maybe as a picture.
- **Daily reminder**: a daily notification with the day's quote, at a time you choose.
- **Settings options**: choose the app's theme and the size of the Arabic text inside the app. Today the Settings tab only shows information. See [Settings](User-Settings.md).
- **More translation languages**: English will stay the default, and you will be able to add other languages you want.
- **Your own Arabic font**: add a font file of your choice for the Arabic text.
- **More ayahs online**: get more ayahs and translations from the internet, in addition to the ayahs that already come with the app.

## Today

Right now the app shows one quote per day on the Home tab, from its 33 ayahs plus any quotes you add. See [The daily quote](User-Daily-Ayah.md).

You can already add your own ayahs, or free text such as a translation in your own language, in the Quotes tab. You can also edit or delete any quote there, including the ayahs that come with the app. See [Your quotes](User-Quotes.md).

Some planned features, like the daily reminder and online ayahs, may need new permissions (for example, to show notifications or to use the internet). Today the app asks for none. See [Privacy and offline use](User-Privacy-And-Offline.md).
