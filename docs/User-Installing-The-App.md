# Installing the App

## Is it on Google Play?

Not yet. The app is not in the Google Play Store at the moment. We will update this page when it is.

## What you need

- An Android phone or tablet.
- Android 7.0 or newer.

To check your Android version, open your phone's **Settings** and look for **About phone**. The name of this menu can be a little different on each phone.

## How to get the app today

There are two ways:

- **Someone gives you the app file.** A person you trust (for example, the developer) can send you the app as an APK file. Open the file on your phone and follow the steps on the screen. Your phone may ask you to allow installing apps from this source. Only install app files from people you trust.
- **You build it yourself.** This is for developers. See the [Developer Setup](Developer-Setup.md) page.

## After installing

- Find **Al Quran Quotes** in your list of apps and tap it.
- The app opens straight to the ayah of the day. There is no sign-up and no account.
- You do not need internet, not even the first time.

## Removing the app

You can uninstall the app like any other Android app. Nothing else needs to be cleaned up, because the app keeps no personal data.
